# Project Charter

## Title
Project Horizon

## Subtitle
Designing Homes That Adapt, Endure and Thrive for the Next 100 Years

## Author
K Ofuafor

## Target Format
Full-colour handbook for Amazon KDP and digital distribution.

## Target Length
250-300 pages for first edition.

## Audience
Self-builders, architects, engineers, passive-house designers, sustainability consultants, and technically minded homeowners.

## Design Principles
1. Structure should outlive technology.
2. Services should be replaceable without damaging the structure.
3. The building envelope should be the primary climate-control system.
4. Every layer should have a clear purpose.
5. Design for inspection, maintenance and replacement.
6. Invest first in building fabric, then mechanical systems.
7. The house should adapt instead of becoming obsolete.
